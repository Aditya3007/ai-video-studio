# AI Video Studio

A production-oriented AI video generation platform that transforms topics or complete stories into polished, series-aware YouTube Shorts (9:16 vertical videos). The platform maintains persistent characters, worlds, and locations across episodes, ensuring visual and narrative continuity.

## Overview

AI Video Studio automates the entire production pipeline:

**Topic or complete story** → **Series-aware story generation** → **Persistent characters/world/locations** → **Screenplay** → **Scenes** → **Shots** → **Character/location visual references** → **Image/video generation** → **Voice/audio** → **Video assembly** → **Quality checks** → **9:16 final video** → **YouTube Shorts publishing**

## Key Features

- **Series Continuity**: Persistent characters, locations, and world-building across episodes
- **Flexible Input**: Topic-to-plot generation or story-to-screenplay adaptation
- **End-to-End Automation**: From story generation to YouTube publishing
- **Provider Flexibility**: Abstracted AI providers for cost optimization and future-proofing
- **Quality Assurance**: AI-powered continuity checking and quality validation
- **Cost Tracking**: Per-task cost estimation and tracking for budget management
